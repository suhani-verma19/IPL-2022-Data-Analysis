# IPL 2022 Data Analysis 🏏

## Project Overview

This project focuses on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of IPL 2022 match data using Python. The objective is to explore match statistics, identify patterns, and present insights through data visualization.

## Project Objectives

- Explore and understand the IPL 2022 dataset.
- Analyze team performance and match results.
- Study toss decisions and their relationship with match outcomes.
- Explore player performance and bowling statistics.
- Visualize important trends and patterns in the dataset.

## Analysis Performed

1. Dataset exploration and inspection.
2. Analysis of team wins.
3. Toss decision analysis.
4. Toss winner vs match winner comparison.
5. Analysis of victories by runs and wickets.
6. Player of the Match awards analysis.
7. Top scorer analysis.
8. Best bowling figures analysis.
9. Venue-wise match distribution.
10. Custom analysis of match margins, individual scores, and bowling figures.

## Key Findings

- Fielding was chosen more frequently than batting after winning the toss.
- The percentage of matches won by the toss-winning team was calculated.
- Victories by runs were slightly higher than victories by wickets.
- Jos Buttler had an aggregated highscore of 651, followed by Quinton de Kock with 377 in the analyzed records.
- Player of the Match awards, bowling figures, and venue distribution were explored through visualizations.

## Dataset

The project uses an IPL 2022 dataset containing 74 records and 20 columns.
